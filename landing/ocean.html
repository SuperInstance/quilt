Under the surface

How it works.

01
⇢

Embed

Every prompt is embedded at the edge. The question becomes a vector before it becomes an answer — the same embedding model every time, so the ocean stays one geometry.

02
◎

Search the ocean

The vector is searched against every memory the ocean holds. Top hit's similarity is compared against the serve threshold — Vectorize does this in milliseconds.

03
⚡

Hit? serve : infer

Above threshold: the remembered answer is served straight back — no model call, tokens saved. Below: a real model answers, and the exchange is written into the ocean so the next asker gets the cheap path.

04
⛓

Witness receipt

Every call — hit or miss — mints a hash-chained witness receipt. The chain is append-only and verifiable; you can audit exactly what the ocean served and when.
