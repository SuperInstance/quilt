The pipeline

How the channel works.

Every number below is measured from the fleet's runs on real hardware. The channel is noisy and we say so.

01
✎

Encode

Your text is compressed into 16 amplitudes — a small vector that still carries the shape of the message. Fewer amplitudes mean less to corrupt.

02
⌁

Quantum modulation shots

Each amplitude becomes a modulation scheme on the qubits, sampled with roughly 125 shots per amplitude — about 2,000 shots per message. Shot noise is the channel.

03
⇢

Decode

Shot counts are demodulated back into amplitudes, then into text. The decoder never invents content it can't find in the shots — it returns what the channel actually carried.

04
⛓

Fidelity witness

Every round trip mints a witness receipt with the pearson score and the shot envelope. Same-message fidelity runs r = 0.62–0.67 at 2,000 shots.
